Ask a Question
Can I order a whole lechon baboy?

Yes, whole lechon baboy inquiries are welcome and are handled by advance order. Please provide your preferred date and let our team confirm availability, size, price, and pickup or delivery arrangements.

How much is lechon belly?

The listed reference price is ₱950 per kilo. Please confirm the current price, available weight, and final total with the team before confirming your order.

Can I order for a party or office event?

Yes, you can inquire about group meals and event orders. Share the event date, estimated number of guests, preferred dishes, and budget so the team can discuss available options.

Is my order confirmed after I submit the form?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to send. Your order is confirmed only after Lola Tinay Lechon replies and confirms the details.
